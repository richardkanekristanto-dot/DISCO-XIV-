What is DISCO?

HIMSI UMN • EST. 2011

A Cherished Tradition Spanning Over 14 Years

D'Information System Community Outbound (DISCO) is the signature annual celebration of HIMSI UMN—a joy-filled tradition strengthening solidarity and heartfelt kinship across the entire Information Systems family, embracing lecturers, alumni, and active students alike!

Founded in 2011 under the name D'Information System Community, DISCO has been lovingly passed down through generations, proudly standing as one of the longest-running and most cherished traditions in HIMSI UMN history.

This year, DISCO XIV gathers us once again at the royal banquet table themed "One Table, Endless Stories" at the highland mountain villa—weaving laughter, outbound camaraderie, and timeless kinship!

To All Guests of Honor

Royal Banquet Invitation

A Royal Banquet Gathering at the Mountain Villa

The extended family of Information Systems, Universitas Multimedia Nusantara, cordially invites all students to partake as honored guests in the grand assembly of DISCO XIV. Continuing the beloved tradition of our annual mountain villa getaway, this year's gathering unfolds in the grandeur of an imperial feast: "One Table, Endless Stories".

Here, every participant is a revered noble, every division serves as a devoted court curator, and every shared triumph weaves enduring kinship beyond generations.
